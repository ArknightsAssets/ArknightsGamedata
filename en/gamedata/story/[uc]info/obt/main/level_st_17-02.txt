The War Casters find the Sarcophagus, but are unable to transport it or put it to use, thus thwarting Evgeny's plans. 'Tulin' reveals her true identity, and she, Koshelna the 'Black Snake,' and Anastasia the 'Princess' all rally their followers in different locations. Scioppa adjusts the ship's heading and prepares to attack Evgeny's flagship.
